ABOUT US

More Than a Gym.
A Legacy of Champions.

Kogan Self-Defense has provided premium Hapkido, Muay Thai, Mixed Martial Arts (MMA), Kung-Fu, Sambo, Combat Sambo and Self Defense instruction in the Chicago area since 1990, with a lineage spanning Grand Masters and multiple generations.

We are an academy of higher learning and standards — a complete martial arts system with an emphasis on the body, mind, and soul. We demand from our students respect, action, bravery, loyalty, trust, humility, integrity and kindness.

Kogan Self-Defense is one of the Chicago area's most innovative Martial Arts and Self Defense Schools. We teach a brand of self-defense that transcends our physical art and includes the self-defense skills of kindness, community and compassion.

FOUNDER
GRAND MASTER · FOUNDER · PUBLISHED AUTHOR

Mikhail Kogan

With over 40 years of studying, practicing, and teaching hand-to-hand combat and martial arts, Grand Master Mikhail Kogan is among the most credentialed martial artists in North America. He personally trained Russia's elite Spetsnaz special forces in hand-to-hand combat, authored a published work on combat methodology, and founded the Spetsnaz USA DART system. He instructs in both English and Russian.
