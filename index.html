OUR STORY

More Than a Gym.
A Legacy of Champions.

Kogan Self-Defense has provided premium Hapkido, Muay Thai, Mixed Martial Arts (MMA), Kung-Fu, Sambo, Combat Sambo and Self Defense instruction in the Chicago area since 1990, with a lineage spanning Grand Masters and multiple generations. Our specialized curricula for ages 5 through adulthood focus on character development, life skills, and self-defense alongside fitness and confidence-building.

We are an academy of higher learning and standards — a complete martial arts system with an emphasis on the body, mind, and soul. We demand from our students respect, action, bravery, loyalty, trust, humility, integrity and kindness. We are more than a gym — we are a teacher's teacher.

Mikhail Kogan

Founder | Grand Master | Published Author

With over 40 years of studying, practicing, and teaching hand-to-hand combat and martial arts, Grand Master Mikhail Kogan is among the most credentialed martial artists in North America.
