OUR STORY

More Than a Gym.
A Legacy of Champions.

Since 1990, Kogan Self Defense has been Chicagoland's most decorated martial arts academy. Founded by Grand Master Mikhail Kogan — a published author, former trainer of Russia's elite Spetsnaz military unit, and four-decade veteran of hand-to-hand combat — our academy is where true warriors are forged.

We are not just a fitness facility. We are an academy of higher learning and standards — a teacher's teacher. Our curriculum encompasses body, mind, and soul, empowering every student to live life fearlessly with honor, character, and purpose.

FOUNDER & GRAND MASTER

Mikhail Kogan

Founder | Grand Master | Published Author

With over 40 years of studying, practicing, and teaching hand-to-hand combat and martial arts, Grand Master Mikhail Kogan is among the most credentialed martial artists in North America. He personally trained Russia's elite Spetsnaz special forces, authored a published work on hand-to-hand combat methodology, and founded the Spetsnaz USA DART system — now practiced by thousands worldwide. He speaks English and Russian and brings the full depth of his four decades of mastery to every class.
